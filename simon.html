How to play

  1. Press New game. Simon lights one pad and plays its note — slowly in the first few rounds, faster as you climb.
  2. Wait for YOUR TURN. The banner turns green and the pads unlock. While Simon plays, the banner says WATCH and the pads stay visibly dimmed and locked, so a tap can’t count as a mistake.
  3. Play it back by pressing the same pads. Get it right and Simon adds one more note.
  4. Made a mistake? In Chill mode Simon slowly replays just the note you missed, outlined on its pad, then it’s your turn again from exactly where you left off. In Strict mode, one wrong pad ends the game.
  5. Colourblind help: leave Labels & patterns on — every pad has a different shape (▲ ● ■ ◆), name, pattern and tone, not colour alone.

A memory trick: say the shapes or colours out loud as Simon plays — “triangle, circle, circle, diamond” — and give the tune a rhythm. Reach round 20 and you’ve beaten Simon.
