How to play

  1. Preview before you press. Hover, focus, or press-and-hold a tile: every tile that would flip gets a dashed green outline and a ghost of its next state. Hold a tile a little longer to lock the preview, then confirm or cancel. Slide off or press Esc to back out.
  2. Press to flip the tile itself and the tiles directly above, below, left, and right.
  3. Read tiles by shape, not just colour. On tiles are filled, hatched, and say ON with a solid disc. Off tiles are plain with a hollow ring and say OFF.
  4. Undo steps back one press at a time — tap it repeatedly to rewind all the way to the starting board.
  5. Hint explains itself. It names a tile from a shortest solution and says why that press helps, not just where to press.

A classic trick: work row by row from the top. To clear a lit tile, press the tile directly below it — the hint will point these chase moves out as they come up.
