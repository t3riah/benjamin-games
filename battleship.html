        How to play
        1. Place your five ships. Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), and Destroyer (2). Drag a ship onto your board — it snaps to the grid and turns red where it won’t fit — or tap a ship, then tap squares. Random fleet places them all at once. In Training, the fleet preset changes the ships, and the preset shown above the board is the one in play.
        2. Press Start battle, then tap any square in Enemy Waters to fire. A burning burst is a hit, a splash ripple is a miss, and a dark wrecked hull means that ship is sunk.
        3. Watch the enemy fleet panel. A ship there shows Sunk only when you sink it, so you cannot see its damage before then.
        4. Optional: turn on Confirm fire and a first tap locks the target — tap the square again (or the Fire button) to shoot, so a stray tap never wastes a shot. The last shot stays pinned at the top, with the full battle log just below it.

        2 Players: pass the device between turns. On your turn you only see your own fleet and your own shots, never the other player’s unhit ships.

        Captain’s tips: ships can’t bend, so once you score a hit, work the squares around it in a line. Stuck? A sonar ping (two per battle) shows expanding sonar rings over one square that definitely holds an enemy ship. On Smart, the computer hunts the same way — spread your ships out.
