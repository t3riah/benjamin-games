        How to play

        1. Place your five ships. Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), and Destroyer (2). Drag a ship onto your board — it snaps to the grid and turns red where it won’t fit — or tap a ship, then tap squares. Random fleet places them all at once.
        2. Press Start battle, then tap any square in Enemy Waters to fire. Green is a hit, a pale square with a dot is a miss, and black means that ship is sunk.
        3. Watch the enemy silhouettes. In the Enemy Fleet panel, each ship’s segments fill in green as you hit it, so you can see at a glance how close you are to sinking it.
        4. Optional: turn on Confirm fire and a first tap locks the target — tap the square again (or the Fire button) to shoot, so a stray tap never wastes a shot. The last shot stays pinned at the top, with the full battle log just below it.

        Captain’s tips: ships can’t bend, so once you score a hit, work the squares around it in a line. Stuck? A sonar ping (two per battle) outlines one square in yellow that definitely holds an enemy ship. On Smart, the computer hunts the same way — spread your ships out.
