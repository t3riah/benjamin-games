How to play

  1. Tap / click a covered square to reveal it. A number means that many mines touch it (including diagonally).
  2. Flag a mine: right-click, long-press, press F while a square is focused, or switch to Flag mode in the banner above the board. On phones there is also a big Flag button pinned at the bottom of the screen, right under your thumb. Flag mode stays on until you switch back, and the banner always shows how many flags you have left.
  3. Chord: click a revealed number when the right number of flags surround it to clear the rest at once.
  4. Big boards: Beginner and Medium always fit your screen — no sideways scrolling. On Expert, use the Zoom controls under the board to enlarge the squares and scroll inside the board area; the mode banner and stats strip stay pinned at the top so mines left is always in view.
  5. Win by revealing every safe square. You don’t have to flag every mine to win. When a game ends, the board shows every mine location and your flag accuracy.
  6. Statistics & badges: tap the bar-chart icon in the header any time to see games played, win %, win streaks, a win-times chart, and your badges for each difficulty. Statistics open on their own when a game ends, and a toast pops up when you unlock a new badge. Everything is saved on this device only.

Start in a corner: corners touch only 3 squares, so the numbers there are easiest to read. If a 1 has only one covered neighbour, that neighbour is a mine — flag it and move on.
