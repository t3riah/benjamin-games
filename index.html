Benjamin's Games

Eighteen games, in the clean NYT Wordle style. Tap a game to play. Every game has its own statistics and badges, saved on your device only.

Buttons

A Lights Out puzzle. Tap a button and it flips itself plus its neighbors. Your goal is to turn every light off, in as few moves as possible.

Boards run from 3 by 3 to 6 by 6, and every board is scrambled from a solved board, so it is always solvable. Preview which tiles will flip before you commit, use multi step Undo, and ask for a Hint that explains why that press helps. A perfect clear, winning in the fewest possible moves, earns a badge.

Play Buttons

Battleship

Place your five ship fleet, then trade shots with the computer on 10 by 10 boards until one fleet is sunk. You win by sinking all five enemy ships before the computer sinks yours.

Drag ships to place them, rotate them, or tap Random fleet. During battle you can turn on Confirm fire so a mis-tap never fires, use two sonar pings per battle to find a guaranteed enemy square, and watch the Enemy Fleet panel fill in as ships are hit and sunk. Three computer difficulties: Easy, Normal, and Smart.

Play Battleship

Simon

Watch four colored pads light up and play tones, then play the sequence back in order. Simon adds one note each round, and you win at round 20.

Play by pad or keyboard. Chill mode gives you 3 lives and replays a missed note slowly, Strict mode ends on one mistake. Playback starts slow and speeds up as rounds climb. Labels and Patterns mode adds a shape and name to each pad for colorblind play.

Play Simon

Minesweeper

Clear every safe square without clicking a mine. Numbers tell you how many mines touch that square, and you flag the squares you think hide mines.

Your first tap is always safe. Beginner is 9 by 9 with 10 mines, Medium is 16 by 16 with 40, Expert is 30 by 16 with 99. Flag by right click, long press, F key, or Flag mode, use chording on numbered squares, and ask for a Hint that pulses a safe square.

Arcade

Five classic games in one place, each with its own statistics, badges, and difficulty options.

  • Hangman: Guess a hidden word one letter at a time, with a hint and a gallows that builds with each wrong guess. Win before the drawing is complete.
  • Wordle: Guess a five letter word in six tries. Green means right letter in the right spot, yellow means right letter in the wrong spot, gray means not in the word.
  • Minesweeper: The same mine clearing game as above, with Easy, Medium, and Hard boards, safe first tap, and flag mode.
  • Tetris: Rotate and stack falling pieces to complete lines. Clear lines to score, level up, and keep the board from filling to the top. Ghost piece and Next preview included.
  • Concentration: Flip cards to find matching pairs. Remember where each card is, match them all in as few moves as you can, on 6, 8, or 12 pair boards.

Arcade 2

Ten more games in one place, each with its own statistics, badges, and Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty.

  • Mastermind: Deduce a hidden color code. Guess, get black pegs for right color in the right spot and white pegs for right color in the wrong spot, and crack it before you run out of guesses.
  • Sudoku: Fill a 9 by 9 grid so every row, column, and 3 by 3 box holds 1 to 9 exactly once. Start from the given numbers, use the number pad, and do not repeat a digit.
  • Snake: Steer a growing snake to eat food and grow longer, without hitting a wall or your own tail. Swipe or use the D-pad, and keep going as it speeds up.
  • Boggle: Find as many words as you can in a letter grid in the time limit, by joining neighboring letters. Longer words score more.
  • Whack-a-Mole: Tap targets the instant they pop up, before they disappear. Fast, accurate taps score, missed or late taps do not.
  • Flood It: Turn the whole board one color. Pick a color, flood from the corner, and absorb neighboring tiles until the board is a single color, within the move limit.
  • Fifteen Puzzle: Slide numbered tiles around the empty space until they read 1 to 15 in order. Every slide counts, so plan ahead.
  • Sokoban: Push boxes onto their target squares. You can push, never pull, and a box stuck in a corner cannot be moved, so think before you push.
  • Maze Runner: Navigate a randomly generated maze from start to finish as fast as you can, without backtracking more than you need to.
  • Rhythm Tap: Tap in time with a beat. Hit on the beat to score, early or late taps score less, and keep the streak going as the tempo changes.
