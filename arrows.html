Monroe's Arrow Escape

Slide every arrow off the board.

How to play

Tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points. If another arrow is in the way, it is stuck — the red path shows you what is blocking it, so try clearing that arrow first. Clear them all and you win. No timer, no lives, just thinking.

Pick a level

Advanced — thin trains

Arrows become little trains of thin squares. Tap any square of a train to slide the whole train out — its head still needs a clear path to the edge.
