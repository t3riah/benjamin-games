# No 13th Floor Games

Live at https://games.no13thfloor.org/

Three small games. Each one is a hands-on way to feel an idea:
resonance, pattern, light.

- **Monromi's Mazes** — lives on its own site at
  https://mazes.no13thfloor.org/. Linked from the hub, not hosted here.
- **Gabriella's Gumdrops** (`gumdrops.html`) — catch falling crystals in
  your basket. Every drop has its own speed and movement, same-color
  catches build a combo, sour drops must be dodged, three lives, levels
  that speed up as you score. Best score is saved on your device
  (`gumdropsBestV1`).
- **Benjamin's Buttons** (`buttons.html`) — a Lights Out puzzle. Turn
  every tile off to light the constellation.

The five games from the earlier hub (Benjamin's Battleship, Monroe's
Arrows, The Quantum Conduit, Fluid Matrix, Rock Paper Scissors) were
retired from the hub on Nov 25, 2026. They are still in this repo's git
history if they are ever wanted back.
